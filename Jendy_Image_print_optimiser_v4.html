Jendy - Image print optimiser v4

Big ideas, sensible files. Your pixels have a chaperone.

Prepare a photo, mosaic or other finished image for printing. Choose the physical print size, adjust cropping and margins, check the available detail, then save a new JPEG or PNG with your preferred file-size target.

Your photos stay yours.Photos you choose remain local on your device. Nothing from your photos is uploaded to a server; processing happens in your browser. Your original files are never changed.

Start here
Choose an image → set print size → review the preview → create and download.

1. Finished image

Start with one finished image from your device. Your original stays untouched while you prepare a new print copy.

One image at a time keeps memory use sane. Your source file is only read, never changed.

2. Print size

Choose the physical dimensions and resolution for your print. DPI means dots per inch; more output pixels cannot restore missing source detail.

When enabled, the app calculates the resulting DPI from the source pixels and selected physical print size.

3. Composition

Decide whether to keep the whole image, crop it to fill the print, or leave a safe margin. Use the preview to check the result.

Scaling below 100% makes the artwork physically smaller inside the same print. It does not reduce output pixels; it adds more surrounding background.

4. Output file

Choose JPEG or PNG and balance image quality with file size. The preferred maximum is a target; check the actual result after creation.

5. Preflight and create

Read the What will happen panel, confirm you have reviewed it, then create your file. Compare the result and download it when ready.
